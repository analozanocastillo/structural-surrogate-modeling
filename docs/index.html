04 / PHYSICAL CHECK

What the measurements
still tell us.

The 34 measured configurations are never used for MLP training or checkpoint selection. We compare their frequency shifts with the FEM and the MLP on the same cases.

ERROR IN PERCENTAGE POINTS (pp)
MLP → measurement = MLP → FEM + FEM → measurement

This identity holds for each signed case error. The mean absolute errors shown above do not add, because opposite signs can partly cancel. Training only on FEM data cannot teach the MLP how the real beam differs from the FEM.

05 / INTERPRETATION

What this experiment supports.

01

LHD helps with a tight budget

At 200 cases, the LHD-trained MLP has lower interior FEM error than the grid-trained MLP.

02

The beam end remains difficult

At 120 cm, the grid-trained MLP performs better at both tested budgets. That position lies outside every training design.

03

Sampling cannot fix physics

Changing FEM training points does not remove the FEM's discrepancy with measurements. That needs a separate calibration study.

Three seeds and five held-out positions make this a controlled comparison of these designs—not a universal claim about LHD. The full results, per-mode values, and methods are in the notebook.
